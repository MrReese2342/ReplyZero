FAQ

A few things worth knowing.

Does ReplyZero reply to comments automatically?

No. ReplyZero helps you find the comments that still need attention. Replies are made on YouTube.

Does ReplyZero modify or delete YouTube content?

No. The current ReplyZero functionality uses YouTube access to identify your channel, retrieve comment threads and replies, and display related video information. It does not post, edit or delete YouTube content.

What happens when I mark a comment as handled?

It moves out of your active inbox so you can keep the queue focused on comments that still need your attention.

Why is there a paid plan?

The Free plan is capped at 20 unlocked unanswered comments. Pro removes that limit for creators with larger backlogs.

Do I need to reconnect every time?

ReplyZero attempts to restore your authorized YouTube session when you reopen the extension. If Google authorization has expired or been revoked, you may be asked to connect again.
