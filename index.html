CREATOR INBOX

Find YouTube comments you forgot to reply to.

ReplyZero is a Chrome extension for YouTube creators. It scans comments on your channel, surfaces comments that have no reply from your channel, and lets you keep track of comments you've handled.

Free: up to 20 unlocked unanswered comments. Pro: all unanswered comments for €5.99/month.

ReplyZero 1.0.0 reads the YouTube data required for the inbox feature. It does not post, edit, or delete comments or other YouTube content.
